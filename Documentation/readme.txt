Top Posters Sidebar Block

It will add a sidebar at your forum with your top posters.

Version: 1.0
Author: borbole
Submitted: 4th November 2012
Last Updated: 4th November 2012

This mod will add a sidebar at your forum with your top posters.

To install the mod upload the plugin file to your forum 's plugin folder.

Then to configure it go to your Acp->Configuration->Settings->Forum Home Options. Scroll all the way to the bottom and you will see 1 extra settings added where you can decide how many posts you would like to show at the Top Posters Block at your sidebar.
